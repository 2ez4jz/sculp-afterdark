# SCULP Studio — independent jury estimate

Estimate, not an official Awwwards score and not affiliated with Awwwards. Independent evidence-only review, 20 October 2026. No calibration offset applied. Every axis started at 6; the evidence below earns the increments.

**The idea, as read from the screen:** A Toronto beauty studio treats makeup and hair as personal editorial choices, inviting visitors to study particular looks, find an artist, and carry a visual reference into an enquiry.

**Weighted overall: 7.73 / 10** (7.7 rounded).

`0.4 × 7.8 + 0.3 × 7.8 + 0.2 × 7.5 + 0.1 × 7.7 = 7.73`

This is numerically above the rubric’s recent Site of the Day reference range threshold; it is not a prediction of an award or a comparison with that day’s submissions.

## Fixes ranked by likely score gain

1. **Carry the close-reading idea consistently through the artist portfolios.** `release-artists/1440-artist-emily.png`, `1440-artist-elaine.png` and `1440-artist-michelle.png` have a photograph with an authored reading; Miranda, Yuki, Mira, Angelina and Giselle first lead into ordinary grids. Give those portfolios one equally specific existing-photo reading with a relevant detail selection. This strengthens the established idea and its content, without adding a different concept. **Creativity / Content / Design.**
2. **Expose the bridal service and price index in the mobile hero.** `release-matrix/390-full-bridal-.png` places “Choose your experience” after the hero, approach and image-story sections. Add a clearly labeled “Compare services & prices” jump beside the initial actions, linking directly to the existing package index; preserve the detailed cards below. **Usability.**
3. **Make saved-photo confirmation readable.** In `release-ui/desktop-selected-photograph.png` and `interactions/mobile-gallery-reference.png`, the dark-gray “Photograph saved” label/check nearly disappears into the dark-green bar. Use the high-contrast light text already established by the primary button and keep a visible check. The user needs to understand that the reference was saved. **Usability / Design / Accessibility.**
4. **Investigate and reduce the recorded long scroll frames.** `release-performance/report.json`: Portfolio cold contains **100 ms**; Modern Chinese warm contains **66.7 and 116.6 ms**; four other warm runs also contain >50 ms intervals. Profile the affected intervals and repeat the same isolated test after a targeted change. The evidence establishes the hitches, not their cause. **Usability / Animations / WPO.**
5. **Restore an optical gap beside Angelina’s name.** `release-artists/375-artist-angelina.png`: the final “a” meets the portrait edge. Reduce only this narrow-screen name size or reserve a small explicit column gap while retaining the portrait pairing. **Design / Responsive.**
6. **Reframe the Modern Chinese “structure” crop around the swept crown.** In `release-matrix/390-full-studies-modern-chinese-.png`, detail 02 mostly shows forehead and an eye while its explanation concerns hair swept away with a controlled crown. Shift or widen that existing crop so the referenced hair is visible. **Content / Design.**
7. **Give testimonials verifiable context where permission allows.** `release-matrix/1440-full-home.png` and `1440-full-bridal-.png` repeat a strong anecdote but identify it only as a Toronto bride/client. Add an approved name or initials, date and source/context; retain anonymous attribution when that is the client’s preference. **Content.**

The report’s FPS field is an integer sample statistic. For transparency, the next column also gives cadence derived from the actual inter-frame intervals: `1000 × count(intervals[1:]) / sum(intervals[1:])`. ## Evidence coverage and limits

- Inspected all **76 current route/width first views**: 44 non-artist views from `release-matrix`, plus the 32 superseding `release-artists` views. Also inspected all 20 supplied full-page captures, both keyboard-first captures, all five interaction captures, both selected-photo desktop captures, every supplied rest/hover pair, all nine performance page captures, and all 19 chronological motion frames plus their supplied before/after/contact views.
- The current `release-ui/report.json` has the required **14 hoverTargets**, all changed. Its screenshots, rather than computed color flags alone, establish the actual hover treatments. No primary unchanged-target defect is evidenced by this collection.
- The static audit covers 18 crawled pages; the raw document proof covers 19; Lighthouse covers **the homepage only**. These scopes are not interchangeable. Automated a11y results are clean, but are not proof of every assistive-technology experience.
- `interactions/report.json` records **21 checks, 0 errors, 1 conversion event**, with all POSTs intercepted and zero emails/customer records created. This is functional test evidence, not proof of a live delivery service.
- `static-proof.json` uses `https://sculpstudio.ca/` for the homepage canonical and the Afterdark host/path for the other documents. Confirm the intended canonical policy and remote OG response at release; the supplied local evidence cannot resolve that deployment decision.
- No source, README, design notes, earlier scores, excluded measurements or web material was inspected. No website files were edited. Motion frames prove changing geometry and state order; they cannot establish display-rate fluidity or subjective motion feel. Sound is unscored. Idle stillness is not penalized.
