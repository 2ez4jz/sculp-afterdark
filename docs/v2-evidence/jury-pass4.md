## Actual interaction and performance findings

The home primary button's full dark fill and the inspection control's full white inversion are meaningful hover treatments. The nav adds an underline and shifts vertically; artist cards visibly scale; the study-detail row changes its whole surface and rotates its mark. Several secondary links use only a small 3 px translation, but that is real positional feedback, not a colour-only change. The selected states remain legible at rest, hover and focus; full-route keyboard screenshots, rather than tightly clipped button captures, establish the surrounding focus rings.

Cadence was recomputed as `1000 / mean(intervals[1:])`, excluding only the initial sampling offset, retaining every subsequent long interval. Current cold samples are approximately 59.6–60.0 fps. Warm samples range from 54.58 to 60.0 fps: Modern Chinese is 54.58, Miranda 58.41, book and occasions 58.81. Modern Chinese retains two intervals above 50 ms, including 116.6 ms. The clean diagnostic traced Modern Chinese summary does not erase that current untraced result; tracing changes measurement conditions and does not identify the cause of the observed warm stall.

## Ranked fixes by likely score gain

1. **Make the existing close-reading composition more specific to each artist.** `artist-readings/1440-{miranda,yuki,mira,angelina,elaine,emily,michelle,giselle}-reading.png` repeats essentially the same photo-plus-two-rows layout. Use the already-established study-page crop treatment to give each artist's highlighted observation a visually appropriate close view and composition, while retaining the same real photograph and selection flow. This develops the idea already on screen and is the largest opportunity in Design (40%) and Creativity (20%). Do not add unrelated spectacle.
2. **Eliminate the measured warm-study scroll stalls.** Target `release-performance/report.json.results[route="studies/modern-chinese/"].warm.intervals`: the actual 66.7/116.6 ms gaps prevent the 8-level smoothness claim. Reproduce that warm path, isolate the work crossing those frames, move or reduce that work, and rerun the same untraced cold/warm sample. The supplied diagnostics do not establish a source-level cause, so naming a particular script, image or CSS property as guilty would be guesswork. This has direct Usability and Animations/Transitions gain.
3. **Give Civil Ceremony the same readable service presentation as the rest of bridal.** In `final-matrix/390-full-bridal-.png` at roughly y=7,400–7,900, the embedded flyer squeezes trial add-ons and conditions to approximately 8–10 px while adjacent service rows read comfortably. Present those existing prices, inclusions and conditions in the same readable responsive type as the other services; retain the flyer as supporting imagery if required. This improves Usability, Content and visual consistency without changing the offering.
4. **Trim repeated closing copy and mobile vertical length.** `390-full-team-.png`, `390-full-about-.png` and `390-full-home.png` repeatedly restate personal beauty/shared standards before similar enquiry/footer blocks. Edit duplicate assertions and reduce the gap after short sections while preserving the strong service, artist and studio facts. In particular, the team page can reach the relevant portfolio links with less repeated explanatory copy. Likely modest Content/Usability gain.
5. **Close the release evidence gaps.** Capture a settled top-of-form brief for every artist: several `artist-readings/390-*-book.png` files are mid-reveal and nearly blank below the intro, unlike the fully readable Miranda/interactions examples. Supply a composed 404 screenshot and chronological back-navigation/mobile transition coverage. Decide the authoritative public host, align the canonical/OG/sitemap policy accordingly, and verify the actual deployed OG URL after release. These are coverage and release-policy tasks, not proof of a current broken route or metadata error.
