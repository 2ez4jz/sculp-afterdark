The three highest-impact fixes:

1. **Resolve the named scroll stalls.** Trace Portfolio, Bridal, Book, Study and desktop Home phases below; split blocking work into short tasks and keep expensive decoding/layout/painting outside active gestures. Require the same complete cold/warm cohorts to demonstrate reliable pacing before claiming smoothness. The packet does not establish a single cause.
2. **Shorten phone price comparison.** In `matrix/390-full-bridal-.png`, retain the six-plan overview but expand the selected plan’s tier block on demand; preserve all 18 prices and exact enquiry prefills. Make the entire section navigation discoverable without clipped trailing labels (`review-clarity/390-no-preference-compare.png`).
3. **Give the studies verified substance.** Add a compact brief, constraints, artist reasoning and client/production outcome to the three study pages, with permissioned credits. Their present detail descriptions are useful but mostly describe the finished photograph.
