# Independent blind jury scorecard — pass 2

**Idea:** SCULP presents personal beauty as a set of choices to look at closely, then lets a visitor carry a chosen look or detail into a conversation with the right artist.

This is an independent evidence-only estimate, not an official award or an award prediction. Every axis starts at 6; the evidence below earns the additions. No calibration offset was applied. No website source, design brief, previous rating, baseline, or external search was used.

## Evidence and cutoff

Reviewed all 76 first-view screenshots in `pass2/` (19 routes at 1440, 768, 390 and 375 px), all ten supplied full-page captures, and `keyboard-first.png`. Reviewed all desktop, mobile, keyboard, reduced-motion, transition and hover screenshots for the **five completed official shoots: home, portfolio, bridal, occasions and team**, with their `report.json` files. The review used contact sheets derived from those images and inspected key originals at full size. Other official page types were still collecting at the review cutoff; no hover or performance claim is extended to those unfinished shoots.

Also reviewed `interactions/report.json` and its three mobile captures, including the regenerated settled booking states; all 16 chronological motion frames in `motion/contact.jpg`, key original frames, before/after images and `motion/report.json`; `final-audit.txt`; `static-proof.json`; and both supplied Lighthouse reports. The screenshot matrix covers all 19 routes, while the audit crawl covers 18 and the independent raw-HTML proof covers 19 including confirmation.

Source: each named `final-shots/<page>/report.json`, `mobile.vitals`, `mobile.scrollFps`, and `desktop.scrollFps`. On a 60 Hz display, integer averages of 59 rather than 60 contain sampling noise; the numbers above are preserved. A 33 ms interval still spans about two refresh intervals. Occasions' 83 ms interval spans about five, and Team's 250 ms interval about fifteen: those material hitches are not rounded away. The reports do not supply a complete dropped-frame count or establish the cause. Desktop results come from a headless software GPU and should not be represented as real-device hardware measurements.

The chronological screencast proves continuous intermediate geometry, not perceived fluidity at full display rate: its 16 capture samples have uneven intervals. Stills alone establish neither motion feel nor timing. `desktop.idleMotion:false` is not penalized; a calm photographic editorial composition does not require perpetual animation.

## Ranked fixes

1. **Remove the Team mobile scrolling hitch first.** `team/report.json mobile.scrollFps` is 49 fps with a 250 ms worst frame; `occasions/report.json` records an 83 ms frame. Profile those exact scroll sections on the same throttle, locate the work coinciding with the long frame, and reduce or schedule that work away from scrolling. Re-measure without rounding the result. This is the largest direct gain across Usability, WPO and Animations.
2. **Complete the two silent primary link treatments.** `bridal/h-03-*` (“Trials & wedding day”) and `team/h-02-*` (“Start an enquiry →”) are identical at rest and hover. Give them the same visible underline/arrow travel or containing treatment used by the answered links, retain keyboard focus, and verify both actual pairs and an empty `hover.noHoverChange`. This raises Design, Usability and Animations with little conceptual change.
3. **Clean the middle of the shared-image transition.** At `motion/frame-007.jpg` the outgoing Mira face lies across the selected moving face; at `frame-009.jpg` the ghost gallery and labels remain. Fade or clip the nonselected gallery before the selected image travels into its destination, while keeping that selected photograph continuous. Verify the same real chronological capture after the change. Do not solve this by replacing the transition with a plain fade.
4. **Make the eight artist openings work harder on a phone.** `375-artist-elaine.png`, `375-artist-emily.png` and `375-artist-michelle.png` devote the first screen largely to the same name, short descriptor, CTA and large gaps. Reduce the gaps around the back link, title and photograph count so the first complete image or artist-specific observation arrives earlier. Preserve the existing typographic hierarchy. This would give the concept more presence outside the three studies and improve comparison speed.
5. **Finish mobile footer and study detail composition.** In `390-full-book-.png` and `390-full-portfolio-.png`, the footer CTA arrow wraps onto a line by itself. Keep the arrow with its phrase or set it deliberately at the opposite edge. In `390-full-studies-modern-chinese-.png`, “The structure” mainly crops the forehead/eyes despite describing the controlled crown; move that crop upward enough to show the hair silhouette being discussed. These are small, concrete craft gains.
6. **Add evidenced story depth to the existing studies.** `1440-full-studies-modern-chinese-.png` explains visual observations well, but the supplied work does not document a client's actual brief or outcome. Where real records permit, add one short brief/constraint, the artist's decision and a specific result or attributed client reflection. Keep the distinction between an editorial reading of a photograph and a documented case study honest. This is the most direct route to a stronger Content score.

No production deployment, real customer submission, backend/email delivery or remote social-image availability is certified by this review. The interaction run explicitly intercepted all POST requests and created zero emails/customer records. A visually designed 404 cannot be assessed from `real404.hasH1` alone at this cutoff.
