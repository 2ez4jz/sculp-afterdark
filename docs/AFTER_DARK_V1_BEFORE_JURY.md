## Ranked fixes

1. **Give the phone's first screen a usable entry point.** In M `390-home.png` and `375-home.png`, the portrait occupies roughly 406 px before a second large text block; no service action is visible at 844 px height. Cap the mobile portrait near 34vh, reduce the following top gap, shorten the introductory paragraph, and bring an existing primary action into that first screen. Preserve the strong desktop split composition.
2. **Make the existing promise visible in the work.** In M `1440-portfolio.png`, remove the duplicated introductory sentence and the sentence about equal visual weight. Use verified artist specialties and service fit to help clients choose. Add concise captions to selected artist images explaining the occasion, the client's brief, and the styling decision; the current first-screen gallery copy does not demonstrate the claimed personal approach. This can raise Content and give the existing idea more specificity without changing the concept.
3. **Complete the primary chooser's feedback.** H `h-01-rest.png` and `h-01-hover.png` are identical. Give the selected “I’m getting married” item a distinct hover response, such as a controlled border reveal or arrow movement, and an equally visible focus state. Preserve the full-surface responses already demonstrated by h-02, h-03, and h-04. Capture route transitions separately before claiming continuity.
4. **Remove the confirmed access friction.** Raise the M-reported 2.04:1, 3.68:1, and 4.01:1 text combinations to at least 4.5:1. Add a first-focus “Skip to content” link, fix the H `keyboard.invisibleFocus` stop, and use a clearly contrasting outline rather than the nearly black brand outline in M `keyboard-first.png`. Increase the 19–23 px menu, inline, and footer targets to at least 24 px; 44 px is preferable for the phone menu.
5. **Finish route metadata and sharing.** M `summary.json` gives every page the same title. Give bridal, occasions, each artist, and enquiry confirmation their own accurate title and description. Add an OG image, Twitter card, and favicon, then verify that the image resolves. Verify the actual site's 404 separately; the supplied fixture audit does not establish a designed error state.
6. **Measure and trim the actual font-loaded path.** Replace the TTF assets reported by A with subset WOFF2 and only the weights used. L `image-delivery-insight` identifies about 31 KiB of avoidable hero-image transfer; provide appropriately sized responsive variants. Rerun throttled mobile LCP with font loading working and investigate the 4.36 s result before claiming the performance target is met.

## Evidence limits

The earlier `before-fonts-matrix` frames were superseded after a capture guard prevented font replay; they were not used for final appearance scores. Corrected M frames resolve their clipping and heavy-font findings. H's failed external-font requests and L's missing external fonts are lab limitations, not proof of a live-site outage; their typography is not used to judge appearance. H's page-navigation scanner did not capture hash-route transitions, which is missing evidence rather than proof that navigation fails. A sums hidden route assets and headings; M verifies one visible H1 per route. A's root robots/sitemap lookup was outside the `/before` fixture and is not counted as an established production defect. Still screenshots cannot establish the feel of motion, and no such quality is assumed.
