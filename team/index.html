Signature Artist / Founder

Miranda

With over 10 years of experience, Miranda brings a background in fashion and celebrity beauty to modern bridal styling. Her work spans international Fashion Weeks, runway shows and advertising campaigns for global brands, giving her bridal work a distinctly editorial eye.

She has also created beauty for luxury destination weddings around the world, working with clients across different cultures and settings to create looks that feel refined, elegant and personal.
